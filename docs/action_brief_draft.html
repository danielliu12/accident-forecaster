Data limitations

Collision observations come from public live traffic feeds (INDOT-managed roads plus probe-data incident detection on local roads); coverage varies by road type and these feeds do not capture every collision. Counts are not adjusted for traffic volume, so higher-volume roads naturally appear more often. Rankings should be read as screening priorities within observed data, not as exposure-adjusted risk rates.

This report summarizes broad, aggregated roadway patterns and conditions. It does not predict individual collisions, identify people involved in collisions, determine the engineering cause of any incident, or replace official traffic, weather, emergency, or transportation guidance.
