The further above that a route sits, the more its problem is a particular place rather than the road as a whole — and the two call for completely different responses.

Extent is the straight-line distance between the outermost collisions we recorded on that route, not the route's true length — a road we only see part of reads short. Per km therefore compares badly between routes of very different extents; the within-route concentration figure does not have that problem, which is why the profiles below lead with it. A ⚠ marks a route we only see in a few disconnected places, where the concentration figure is an artefact of the gaps rather than a finding about the road.
