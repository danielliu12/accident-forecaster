This period's summary

Our roadway monitoring has identified 4 locations across 2 Indiana counties where collisions keep happening at the same spot (analysis period: 2026-08-04 to 2026-08-14). When crashes repeat at one location, involving different drivers on different days, the pattern usually points to something about the road itself — and that is usually fixable.
