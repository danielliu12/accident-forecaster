ReadHow to read this page

A quiet location is not a safe location. We can only see collisions that reach a public traffic feed. Coverage is strongest on interstates and state-managed roads and thinnest on local streets, so a road's absence from this page says as much about our visibility as about the road.

These are patterns, not causes. We can show that collisions repeat at a place. Determining why takes a site visit, traffic counts, and engineering judgement — which is exactly what the companion brief asks public agencies to do.

Nothing here describes an individual collision. No people, vehicles, injuries, or fault. Counts, dates, and places only.

About this report. This report summarizes broad, aggregated roadway patterns and conditions. It does not predict individual collisions, identify people involved in collisions, determine the engineering cause of any incident, or replace official traffic, weather, emergency, or transportation guidance.
